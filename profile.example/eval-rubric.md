## Worth applying? Alex's own criteria (FICTIONAL EXAMPLE)

Shown next to each job on the labelling screen (`/label?set=<name>`). The question is "should I have seen this as worth applying to?", judged on the job text alone.

**Yes** when all of these hold:
- A senior PM or product owner role (not engineering, not sales).
- Energy software, logistics, IoT platforms or AI products, or a domain where operations-heavy B2B customers are the users.
- Remote in Europe, or hybrid in Valencia or Madrid.
- No hard gate: no German, French or Dutch requirement, not betting, adtech or defence, not US-only remote.

**No** when any hard gate applies, the role is junior, or the domain has nothing to do with the work above.

**Unsure** when one fact decides it and the text does not say it (remote scope, required language, seniority). Write which fact is missing.

Pay below EUR 60,000 is a No only when the posting states it.
